Every post on this site carries a stamp: the hours I had played, my battle points, and my PORC rank at the time of writing. The stamp comes from the nearest checkpoint in the tracker, so later, when I read these back, I know exactly how much I did and didn't know.

## What goes here

- Match reflections and what I changed after them
- Structure experiments, footwork drills, things that worked and things that hurt
- Milestones on the climb

## The rule

Write it down while it is still fresh. The version of me at hour 300 will not remember what hour 90 felt like.
